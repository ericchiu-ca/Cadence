# Privacy Policy

*Cadence: Offline Music — last updated October 10, 2026*

Cadence is a music player for files you already own. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not collect any information about you.

## What stays on your device

- Your music files, playlists, favourites, play counts, settings and lyrics cache are stored in the app on your device.
- If you keep your library in iCloud Drive, the files sync through **your own** iCloud account. Apple handles that sync; the developer cannot see it.

## What leaves your device

- **Lyrics.** When you open lyrics for a song that has none of its own, Cadence asks [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net), a free and open lyrics database, for them. The request contains the song's title, artist, album and length — nothing that identifies you or your device. LRCLIB's own terms apply to that request.
- **Artist information (optional).** Builds compiled with Spotify or Discogs API keys look up artist names and pictures from those services. The version on the App Store is built without them and makes no such requests.

Nothing is sent anywhere else, and nothing is sold or shared.

## Permissions

- **Files you open from other apps** are read only to play them and to read their tags and artwork.
- **Siri** is used only when you ask Siri to play something in Cadence.

## Children

Cadence does not collect information from anyone, including children.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at <https://github.com/ericchiu-ca/Cadence/issues>.

# 隐私政策

*Cadence: Offline Music —— 更新于 2026 年 10 月 10 日*

Cadence 是一款播放你自己音乐文件的播放器。没有账号、没有统计分析、没有广告，也不做任何追踪。开发者不收集关于你的任何信息。

## 保存在你设备上的内容

- 你的音乐文件、播放列表、喜欢的歌曲、播放次数、设置和歌词缓存都保存在你设备上的应用里。
- 如果你把音乐库放在 iCloud 云盘，文件通过**你自己的** iCloud 账户同步，由 Apple 负责，开发者无法看到。

## 会离开你设备的内容

- **歌词。** 当你为一首没有内嵌歌词的歌打开歌词时，Cadence 会向免费开放的歌词库 [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net) 查询，请求里只包含歌名、歌手、专辑和时长，不含任何能识别你或你设备的信息。该请求适用 LRCLIB 自己的条款。
- **艺人资料（可选）。** 使用 Spotify 或 Discogs 密钥编译的版本会向这些服务查询艺人名称和图片。App Store 上的版本不带这些密钥，不会发出此类请求。

除此之外不会向任何地方发送数据，也不会出售或分享任何数据。

## 权限

- **从其他应用打开的文件**只用于播放，以及读取其标签和封面。
- **Siri** 只在你让 Siri 用 Cadence 播放时使用。

## 儿童

Cadence 不收集任何人的信息，包括儿童。

## 联系方式

对本政策有疑问，请在 <https://github.com/ericchiu-ca/Cadence/issues> 提交 issue。
